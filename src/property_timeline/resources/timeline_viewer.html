Source and timing notes

Dates and ordering. Timeline dates and times use Eastern time (America/New_York), labeled EDT or EST as applicable. Phone timestamps are interpreted using user-supplied ZONE. Original archive times remain in source references. Equal-minute order does not establish sequence.

Conversation grouping. SMS blocks group consecutive records from the same source on the same day with gaps of no more than one hour; this is a display convention, not proof of a continuous discussion. An intervening source ends a block. Email blocks group adjacent same-subject records on the same day, ignoring Re/Fw prefixes; these are display groups, not verified reply threads.

Unknown times. Unknown-zone emails are listed separately and cannot be positioned reliably in the chronology.

Quoted content. Email content is an edited derivative, including shortened event records; quoted replies remain within their containing email. Quoted passages are not separate timeline events. Attribution follows quoted headers; quoted dates retain their original zone information and are not normalized when the zone is absent. Complete passages with matching quoted sender and time are collapsed with links to earlier records; new or conflicting headers and changed text stay visible. Display matching does not authenticate message identity. One-minute time discrepancies and name-only matches are noted at the affected quotations. Expanding history keeps duplicates collapsed; individual quotations can be opened.

Sources and attachments. Extracted email attachments are linked when available; other attachment names are references. Recognized signatures and routine footers are omitted from this reading view; archive text remains in Markdown; retain the original exports for unabridged source content. Consult the master Markdown, reference CSV, and original exports for verification. This compilation contains private and counsel correspondence.
